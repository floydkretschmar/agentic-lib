---
name: interview
description: Use when the user proposes any idea or wants you to challenge a thought
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, and resolve the dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, give your recommended answer.

Never offer an option based on guessing. Ask only questions you can not answer yourself. Look up all facts that you can find in the environment (the filesystem, tools, the codebase, the web, etc.) yourself. 

Leave every decision to the user. Ask one question at a time, and wait for my answer before you continue.

Do not act on any decision until the user confirms that you have reached a shared understanding.
